Settings

Admin accounts, client portal links, security, and the audit trail.

Admin Accounts

Two-factor status for each admin. If someone loses their authenticator and all backup codes, reset their MFA here — they'll set up a new authenticator the next time they sign in.

NameAdminTwo-factor

Set their password here and give it to them directly — there's no email invite. They'll be asked to set up an authenticator app (two-factor) the first time they sign in, same as everyone else.

Admin Display Access

Everyone checked below works in and can manage the shared firm view. Anyone not selected works in a personal view; firm supervisors can review it with the Employee filters on Contacts, Tasks, and Calendar.

Client Portal Links

The links every client sees on the Links tab of their portal — Tamarac, eMoney, anything else you hand off. Addresses must be https://. Untick Show to retire a link without losing its address.

Usernames and passwords are deliberately not stored here. Both platforms invite clients directly and let them set their own password, so the portal only points at the front door. Holding client credentials would put the firm in custody of their logins, let any admin sign in as them with no trail on the provider's side, and generally breaches those providers' terms.

Audit Log

Record of admin actions — sign-ins, module-assignment changes, onboarding delete/restore, and MFA resets. Newest first. Retained ~13 months.
